M.S. student in Health Informatics, Yale University

About Me
======

I am an M.S. student in Health Informatics at the Yale School of Public Health and hold a B.S. in Statistics and Psychology with Highest Distinction from the University of Virginia. My research interests center on applying data science, biostatistics, and bioinformatics to better understand mental health and its complex biological, behavioral, and social determinants.

My research integrates neuroimaging, electronic health records, wearable sensing, genetic data, advanced statistical methods, and AI/deep learning.

I plan to apply to Ph.D. programs in biostatistics, health/bioinformatics, and related fields for Fall 2027. My long-term goal is to develop rigorous, interpretable, and clinically meaningful data science methods that advance mental health research and improve care.
